# Project Kickoff — Theme Specification

> A PPTX template. The attached reference.pptx IS the visual system — clone it; do not restyle it.

## How to use the reference

1. Open the attached `reference.pptx` with the officecli skill.
2. Keep its slide dimensions, white background, teal accent bars, fonts (Calibri body, Consolas labels) and slide-number chrome exactly as they are.
3. Replace placeholder text with the user's content; duplicate content slides as needed; delete unused slides.
4. Palette (do not change): white `#ffffff` background, graphite `#1f2933` text, teal `#0e7c7b` accent, muted `#7b8794` secondary.

## Content structure

1. Title slide — project, date, sponsor
2. Why this project — problem, cost of doing nothing, opportunity
3. Scope & deliverables — in/out of scope, acceptance criteria
4. Team & responsibilities — roles and decision rights
5. Timeline & milestones — phases with dates
6. Risks & next steps (optional)

## Voice

Direct and operational. Every commitment has an owner and a date. Do not invent facts to fill slots.
