# Business Review — Theme Specification

> A PPTX template. The attached reference.pptx IS the visual system — clone it; do not restyle it.

## How to use the reference

1. Open the attached `reference.pptx` with the officecli skill.
2. Keep its slide dimensions, background fills, accent bars, fonts (Calibri body, Consolas labels) and slide-number chrome exactly as they are.
3. Replace the placeholder text content slide by slide with the user's content; duplicate a content slide when more slides are needed; delete unused slides.
4. Palette (do not change): navy `#0b1f3a` background, white `#ffffff` text, amber `#f2a33c` accent, muted `#9db0c9` secondary.

## Content structure

1. Title slide — company, quarter, presenter
2. Executive summary — ≤3 sentences
3. KPI scorecard — 3–4 headline metrics with deltas
4. Segment results — one block per segment: result, driver, action
5. Priorities & decisions — max 3, each with owner and date
6. Outlook (optional) — risks and guidance

## Voice

Board-meeting register: numbers first, adjectives last. Every metric carries a period-over-period delta. Do not invent facts to fill slots — leave a clearly marked placeholder if data is missing.
