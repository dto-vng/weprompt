# IT service catalogue

Maintained by the Infrastructure team. Every service below has a named owner, a request route, and a stated turnaround. If a request does not appear here, it goes to the general support channel and Infrastructure decides where it belongs.

## How to read this catalogue

Turnaround is measured in working days from the moment the request has everything it needs, not from the moment it was raised. An incomplete request is returned the same day with a note saying what is missing.

Approval means a written approval recorded against the request. A verbal approval in a meeting is not an approval for the purposes of this catalogue, and Infrastructure will ask for it in writing before starting work.

## Accounts and identity

### Directory account

Owner: People Operations. Route: the joiner request raised by People Operations. Turnaround: three working days. Approval: none beyond the signed offer.

A directory account is created disabled and enabled on the start date. Enabling early requires an approval from the hiring manager and is logged.

### Mail account and distribution lists

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: support channel. Turnaround: one working day for a mailbox, same day for list membership. Approval: the list owner for any list that reaches more than fifty people.

Shared mailboxes are owned by a named person, never by a team alias. When that person leaves, the mailbox is reassigned as part of the leaver checklist or it is archived.

### Service accounts

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: support channel with a written purpose. Turnaround: five working days. Approval: the owning team's manager.

Service accounts do not get interactive sign-in and are reviewed every six months. A service account with no recorded traffic for two review cycles is disabled.

## Devices

### Laptop replacement

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: support channel. Turnaround: five working days in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, ten working days elsewhere. Approval: the hiring manager for anything above the standard build.

The standard refresh is every three years. An earlier replacement needs a fault report, not a preference.

### Monitors and peripherals

Owner: Facilities. Route: office stock request. Turnaround: same day when in stock. Approval: none up to two items, then the team manager.

### Mobile devices

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: support channel. Turnaround: ten working days. Approval: the team manager and Finance Operations, because the cost lands on the team's own cost centre.

A company mobile device is enrolled in management before handover. An unenrolled device is not given access to company mail.

## Network and remote access

### Office wired network

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: support channel. Turnaround: two working days. Approval: none for staff ports, the Infrastructure lead for anything in a meeting room.

### Corporate VPN

Owner: Information Security. Route: support channel. Turnaround: two working days. Approval: see the internal information-security policy — the catalogue does not restate approval authority for corporate VPN, because that policy is the single source of truth and has changed twice this year.

### Partner VPN gateway

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: contract manager. Turnaround: five working days. Approval: the contract manager plus Information Security sign-off on the service list.

## Repositories and environments

### Repository access

Owner: Platform team. Route: support channel naming the repositories. Turnaround: two working days. Approval: the repository owner.

Organisation-wide read access is not a thing anyone can request. Access is per repository, and a request naming more than ten repositories is sent back to be justified line by line.

### Staging environment

Owner: Platform team. Route: support channel. Turnaround: three working days. Approval: the team manager.

Staging data is a scrubbed copy refreshed weekly. Copying production data into staging by hand is not permitted under any circumstance.

### Production access

Owner: Information Security. Route: access review, not the support channel. Turnaround: the next scheduled review, which runs fortnightly. Approval: Information Security and the service owner.

Production access is time-bound. Every grant carries an expiry, and there is no permanent grant for an individual.

## Software

### Standard software

Owner: Infrastructure. Route: self-service portal. Turnaround: immediate. Approval: none.

### Paid software and subscriptions

Owner: Finance Operations. Route: purchase request. Turnaround: ten working days. Approval: the team manager, then Finance Operations above five million VND a year.

A subscription bought on a personal card is not reimbursed. This is the most common rejected expense line in the quarter.

## Escalation

If a request sits beyond its stated turnaround, escalate to the named owner first and to the Infrastructure lead second. Escalating to both at once is not faster and makes the trail harder to read.
